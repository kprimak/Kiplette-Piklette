About Pickleball

The Fastest Growing Sport in America

Pickleball combines the best of tennis, badminton, and ping-pong into one addictive game. With 36 million players and counting, there's never been a better time to pick up a paddle.

Quick to Learn

Most beginners can rally within 30 minutes. The smaller court and underhand serve make it incredibly accessible for all ages and skill levels.

Social & Fun

Pickleball is inherently social. Whether you're playing singles or doubles, the compact court keeps players close and conversations flowing.

Great Exercise

A typical game burns 400–600 calories per hour. Low-impact on joints with high cardiovascular benefit — perfect for players of any fitness level.

Competitive Scene

From local rec leagues to national USAPA tournaments, pickleball offers a growing competitive ladder for players chasing their next level.
