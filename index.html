About Us

The Game,
Refined

"We refused to accept that high-performance had to look like a neon advertisement."

When we looked at the current landscape of pickleball, we saw a sport with immense potential but a visual identity that felt adrift. While pickleball was celebrated as the modern evolution of tennis, it seemed to have traded the court's historic elegance for neon palettes, frantic patterns, and tacky designs.

We wanted something different — something classy, chic, and timeless. And that's how we founded Kiplette, because for us, the game is not merely about the rally. It is about the poise you carry while playing it.

About Pickleball

North America's
Fastest Growing Sport

Pickleball merges the timelessness of tennis, the precision of badminton, and the intimacy of ping-pong. With 36 million players and a burgeoning professional circuit, there has never been a finer moment to step onto the court.

01

Swift to Master

Most players rally within 30 minutes of their first session. The underhand serve and compact court make the sport immediately accessible to all ages and skill levels.

30 min to first rally
02

Inherently Social

The compact court keeps players close. Whether singles or doubles, pickleball fosters a unique camaraderie rarely found in other racquet sports.

4 players per court
03

Peak Conditioning

400–600 calories per hour. Low joint impact with high cardiovascular return — the ideal sport for sustained fitness and longevity at any level.

600 cal/hr average burn
04

A Growing Circuit

From local club leagues to USAPA national championships, the competitive ladder rewards dedication and welcomes players of every ambition.

36M players worldwide
