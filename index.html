About Pickleball

America's Fastest
Growing Sport

Pickleball merges the elegance of tennis, the precision of badminton, and the intimacy of ping-pong. With 36 million players and a burgeoning professional circuit, there has never been a finer moment to step onto the court.

01

Swift to Master

Most players rally within 30 minutes of their first session. The underhand serve and compact court make the sport immediately accessible to all ages.

02

Inherently Social

The compact court keeps players close. Whether singles or doubles, pickleball fosters a unique camaraderie rarely found in other racquet sports.

03

Peak Conditioning

400–600 calories per hour. Low joint impact with high cardiovascular return — the ideal sport for sustained fitness at any level.

04

A Growing Circuit

From local club leagues to USAPA national championships, the competitive ladder rewards dedication and welcomes players of all ambitions.
